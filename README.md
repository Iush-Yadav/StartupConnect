# 🚀 StartupConnect

**StartupConnect** is a collaborative web platform that bridges the gap between **startup entrepreneurs** and **investors** — providing a space to share ideas, discover potential ventures, and collaborate meaningfully.

---

## 🌟 Inspiration

Startups often lack visibility and early investor traction. Meanwhile, investors are always on the lookout for promising innovations. **StartupConnect** was born to solve this — offering a space where ideas and funding can meet with ease.

---

## 🔧 Features

- 🧠 **Idea Sharing** – Startups can showcase their vision and pitch publicly.
- 🔐 **Secure Profiles** – Both founders and investors have authenticated profiles.
- 🫱‍🫲 **Collaboration** – Entrepreneurs and investors can network directly.
- 💬 **Messaging** – Simple communication to discuss ideas or funding rounds.
- 📱 **Fully Responsive** – Smooth experience across mobile, tablet, and desktop.

---

## 🛠️ Built With

- **Frontend:** React.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion
- **Backend/Auth:** Supabase (Database + Auth)
- **Deployment:** Vercel
- **Version Control:** GitHub
